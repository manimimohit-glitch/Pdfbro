PDF analyzer

Drop a PDF.See what’s in it.

Clearpane reads the text, ranks the terms that matter, pulls out amounts and dates, and shows how much each page carries. It all runs in your browser, so the file stays on your device.

      Find in document

      Ask the document

      The PDF is split into small overlapping chunks, the best-matching chunks are found, and a small model running in your browser writes the answer from them. Loading the model also adds meaning-based reranking.

      1

      Extract

      PDF.js pulls the text layer from every page, inside your browser tab.

      2

      Rank

      Common words are dropped, the rest are counted, and plurals are merged.

      3

      Select

      Sentences carrying the most top terms form the summary. Questions reuse the same text in small chunks for an optional local model. Nothing is uploaded.

      Clearpane reads text, not images, so scanned PDFs without a text layer cannot be analyzed. Reading ease uses an English formula. PDF rendering and text extraction by PDF.js. The optional answer and search models are fetched from Hugging Face the first time you load it and then cached by your browser. Your document never leaves the device.
